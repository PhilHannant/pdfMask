How to redact a bank statement or payslip

Letting agents, landlords, mortgage brokers, accountants and new employers sometimes ask for a bank statement or payslip. They may only need to see part of it. Here's how to hide the rest so it's properly gone, without uploading your finances to a website.

Ask first what they need to see. If a document is being used to check your identity, income or address, removing certain details might mean it can't be accepted. It's worth a quick question to whoever asked for it.

What people usually hide, and what they leave

It depends on who's asking and why. As a rough guide:

Often left visible

Your name and address, the bank's name, the statement period, and the lines they've asked about, such as salary payments or rent.

Often hidden

Everyday spending, who you've paid or been paid by, card numbers, and all but the last few digits of your account number.

On payslips

People often look at their National Insurance number, employee or payroll number, tax code, and pension or deduction details.

Why a black box in a PDF editor isn't enough

Statements downloaded from online banking are usually "real" PDFs with selectable text. If you draw a black rectangle over a transaction in a typical PDF editor, or use the markup pen on your phone, the transaction is still in the file underneath. Anyone can select it and copy it out, and some apps let you simply delete the box.

pdfMask rebuilds each page you redact so the hidden details are removed from the file itself. If you've already redacted a statement some other way, you can check it for hidden text.

Why not just use any online PDF editor?

A bank statement is one of the most revealing documents you own: where you live, where you shop, who you pay and how much you earn. Most online PDF tools upload your file to their servers to edit it.

pdfMask does all the work in your browser instead, so your statement never leaves your device.

Frequently asked questions

Will a redacted statement still be accepted?

That's up to whoever asked for it. Some are happy with spending hidden; others need the full document, particularly for identity or anti-money-laundering checks. Ask them if you're not sure.

Can I redact a screenshot or a scanned statement?

pdfMask works with PDFs. If you have a scan saved as a PDF, it works the same way. For a photo or screenshot, save it as a PDF first, for example with your phone's print or share option.

Will the redacted file look different?

Pages you redact are turned into images, so they look the same but their text can no longer be selected. Pages you don't touch stay exactly as they were.
