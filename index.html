Why use a PDF tool that doesn't upload your files?

Most online PDF tools send your document to their servers to process it. For contracts, payslips, bank statements, medical letters or client case files, that means handing personal data to a company you know little about.

pdfMask works differently. The tools load into your browser once, then do all the work on your device. Nothing you open is uploaded, stored or logged.

Redaction that actually removes the content

Drawing a black box over a PDF, or highlighting text in black, only hides it on screen. The words are still in the file, and anyone can copy them out. It's one of the most common ways confidential information leaks.

pdfMask's redactor rebuilds each page you redact so the marked content is gone from the file, not just covered up. If you've received or produced a redacted PDF elsewhere, the redaction checker will show you whether anything is still hiding underneath.

Built for UK legal, finance and HR teams

Solicitors, accountants, bookkeepers and HR teams handle personal data every day. Because pdfMask never receives your documents, it doesn't add another place your clients' information is sent.

Frequently asked questions

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

No. pdfMask runs entirely in your web browser. Your PDFs are read and processed in your device's memory and are never sent to our servers or anyone else's.

Is the redaction permanent, or just a black box on top?

It's permanent. Simply drawing a black box over a PDF leaves the text underneath in the file, where anyone can copy it. pdfMask instead rebuilds every page you redact as a flat image with the marked areas blacked out, so the hidden text, images and data are removed from the file entirely. Pages you don't redact are kept exactly as they were.

Is pdfMask free?

Yes. All the tools are free to use, with no sign-up, no watermarks and no file limits beyond what your browser can handle.

How does pdfMask fit with UK GDPR?

pdfMask never receives your documents. They're processed on your own device, and nothing is uploaded, stored or logged. That sits well with keeping personal data to as few places as possible, though your organisation remains responsible for how it handles personal data.

Do I need to install anything?

No. pdfMask works in any modern browser on Windows, Mac, Linux, iPhone and Android.
