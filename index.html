Why use a PDF tool that doesn't upload your files?

Most online PDF tools send your document to their servers to process it. For contracts, payslips, bank statements, medical letters or client case files, that means handing personal data to a company you know little about.

pdfMask works differently. The tools load into your browser once, then do all the work on your device. Nothing you open is uploaded, stored or logged.

Frequently asked questions

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

No. pdfMask runs entirely in your web browser. Your PDFs are read and processed in your device's memory and are never sent to our servers or anyone else's.

Is pdfMask free?

Yes. All the tools are free to use, with no sign-up, no watermarks and no file limits beyond what your browser can handle.

Does pdfMask help with UK GDPR?

Because files never leave your device, pdfMask does not collect, store or transfer the personal data inside your documents. That avoids adding a third-party processor for the file itself, though your organisation remains responsible for its own UK GDPR compliance.

Do I need to install anything?

No. pdfMask works in any modern browser on Windows, Mac, Linux, iPhone and Android.
