Merge PDF files without uploading them

Combine contracts, invoices, statements or scanned pages into a single PDF. Everything happens in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

Merge your PDFs

Choose two or more PDFs. They're joined in the order shown below.

    How to merge PDFs

    1. Click Choose files and select all the PDFs you want to combine. Hold Ctrl (or Cmd on a Mac) to pick several at once.
    2. Check the order in the list. To change it, choose the files again in the order you want.
    3. Click Merge and Download. The combined PDF is saved straight to your device.

    Private by design

    Most online tools upload your files to their servers to merge them. pdfMask does the work in your browser instead, so bank statements, contracts and client documents are never sent anywhere.

    Need to remove something before you combine files? Redact the PDF first, then merge the redacted copy.

    Good to know

    • Pages keep their original quality and text. Nothing is converted or compressed.
    • Password-protected or restricted PDFs need unlocking first. Use Unlock PDF, then merge the unlocked copies.
    • Very large files depend on your device's memory, but most everyday documents merge in a few seconds.
