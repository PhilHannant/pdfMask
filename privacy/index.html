Privacy

The short version: your documents never leave your device, and pdfMask collects as little as a website can.

Your documents

The PDFs you open in pdfMask are processed in your own browser. They are never uploaded, so pdfMask never receives your files, their names, their contents or any passwords you type to unlock them. There is nothing on a server to store, share or lose.

The security page explains how this works and how to check it yourself.

What the site does collect

Hosting

pdfMask is hosted by Cloudflare. As with any website, the host handles technical details of each request, such as your IP address and browser type, in order to deliver the page and protect the site from abuse. Cloudflare's privacy policy explains how it handles that information.

Page-view counts

The information pages, such as the home page and the guides, use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It records things like which page was viewed, the site you came from, your country and your type of browser and device. Cloudflare says it does this without cookies and without tracking individual visitors across sites.

It doesn't run on the tool pages, where you open files. Their security policy blocks it.

Email

If you email hello@pdfmask.co.uk, I receive your email address and whatever you write. I use it to reply to you, and I don't pass it on.

What the site doesn't do

  • No accounts. There's nothing to sign up for.
  • No adverts or advertising trackers.
  • No cookies of its own. pdfMask doesn't set any cookies or use your browser's storage to remember you. Cloudflare, as the host, may set a short-lived security cookie if it needs to check that a visitor isn't an automated bot.
  • No selling or sharing of data. There's very little to share in the first place.

Who is responsible

pdfMask is run by Phil Hannant in the UK. If you have a question about your information, or want a copy of anything held about you, email hello@pdfmask.co.uk.

If you're unhappy with a reply, you can raise it with the UK's Information Commissioner's Office.
