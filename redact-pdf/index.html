  1. Choose your PDF above. It opens in your browser and is never uploaded.

Why a black box isn't enough

Most PDF editors let you draw a black rectangle or highlight text in black. That changes how the page looks, but the original text is still inside the file. Anyone can select it, copy it, or search for it. Redaction failures like this regularly make the news, from court filings to government reports.

pdfMask avoids this by rebuilding every page you redact. The page is redrawn as a flat image with your marked areas painted solid black, and that image replaces the original page. The hidden text, fonts and form values on the page are gone from the file, so there's nothing left to copy out.

What you can redact

Personal details

Names, addresses, dates of birth, phone numbers, National Insurance numbers and signatures.

Financial information

Account and sort codes, card numbers, salaries, transactions and balances.

Anything else on the page

Photos, logos, handwriting, form entries and scanned content. If you can see it, you can remove it.

Good to know

  • Redacted pages become images, so their text can't be selected or searched afterwards. Pages you don't redact keep their normal text.
  • The file gets a little bigger, typically a few hundred kilobytes to around a megabyte per redacted page.
  • You get a fresh file. The original's title, author, bookmarks, attachments and form fields aren't carried over.
  • Check before you send. Make sure you've marked every instance of the information, including in headers, footers and page numbers.

Frequently asked questions

Is the redaction permanent, or just a black box on top?

It's permanent. Drawing a black box over a PDF leaves the text underneath in the file, where anyone can copy it. pdfMask rebuilds every page you redact as a flat image with the marked areas blacked out, so the hidden text, images and data are removed from the file entirely. Pages you don't redact are kept exactly as they were.

Why can't I select the text on redacted pages any more?

Each redacted page is turned into an image so that nothing from the original page can survive inside the file. The trade-off is that text on those pages can no longer be selected or searched. Pages without redactions keep their normal, selectable text.

Does the redacted file keep the original's title and author?

No. pdfMask creates a fresh file, so the original's document properties, bookmarks, attachments and form fields aren't carried over.

Can I redact a password-protected PDF?

If the PDF needs a password to open, remove the password first using the software you normally open it with, then redact the unlocked copy.
