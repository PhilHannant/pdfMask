Unlock a PDF without uploading it

Remove restrictions that stop you printing, copying or editing a PDF. Everything happens in your browser, so your document never leaves your device, and its text, links and bookmarks are kept.

Unlock your PDF

Choose a PDF. If it needs a password to open, you'll be asked for it.

How to unlock a PDF

  1. Choose your PDF above. It's checked in your browser and never uploaded.
  2. If it only has restrictions, click Unlock & Download. If it needs a password to open, enter the password first.
  3. A new copy is saved to your device with -unlocked added to the name. Your original file isn't changed.

What gets unlocked

Printing

Print the document, or print it at full quality where only low-resolution printing was allowed.

Copying

Select and copy text and images into other documents.

Editing and forms

Fill in forms, add comments, and edit, merge or redact the document in other tools.

Restrictions or a password to open?

PDFs can be protected in two ways. Some open normally but block printing, copying or editing. Unlock removes those restrictions without needing anything from you.

Others need a password before they'll open at all. If you know the password, enter it and pdfMask saves a copy that opens without one. See removing a password from a PDF for more. pdfMask can't open a PDF if you don't know its password, and it doesn't try to guess it.

Only unlock documents you own or have permission to use.

Good to know

  • The first unlock takes a moment longer while your browser downloads the unlock engine (about 2 MB). After that it's cached.
  • Everything else is kept: text stays selectable and searchable, and links, bookmarks and form fields are preserved.
  • Need to redact or merge a locked PDF? Unlock it here first, then use Redact PDF or Merge PDFs.

Frequently asked questions

What are PDF restrictions?

Some PDFs open normally but stop you printing, copying text, editing or filling in forms. The author sets these with a permissions password. Unlock saves a copy without them.

Can pdfMask crack a PDF password?

No. If a PDF needs a password to open, you have to enter the correct password. pdfMask then saves a copy that opens without it. It doesn't guess or crack passwords.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Unlock runs entirely in your browser. The first time you use it, your browser downloads the unlock engine (about 2 MB); after that, your file is processed on your device and never sent anywhere.

What types of protection does it support?

Standard PDF password protection, including 40-bit and 128-bit RC4 and 128-bit and 256-bit AES encryption. PDFs protected with digital certificates or rights-management software aren't supported.
